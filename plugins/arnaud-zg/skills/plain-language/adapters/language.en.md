# language · en

**ASD-STE100 is written for English.** Its writing rules apply as they are.

- Use the simple tenses: "The script reads the file", not "The script will be reading the file".
- Use a verb, not a noun made from it: "Check the file", not "Do a check of the file".
- Keep "please", "could you" and "thanks" for the light level only.
- Prefer the short word: "use", not "utilise" or "leverage". "Start", not "initiate".
