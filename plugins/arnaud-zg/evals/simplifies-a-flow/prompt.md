Our expense refund flow: the employee fills a 14-field form, prints it, gets a manager signature,
scans it, emails it to finance, finance re-types it into the accounting tool, a second finance
person approves anything over 50 euros, and the employee gets an email when it is paid, about three
weeks later. Make it radically simpler.
